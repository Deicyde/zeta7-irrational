# Real determinant bound

This chapter bounds the normalized determinant at zeta(7) from above.
Andreief's identity rewrites it as a multiple integral with a squared
Vandermonde factor; after scaling, the exponent is a discrete
logarithmic energy in an explicit external field. That energy is
bounded for every configuration by comparison against an explicit
sixteen-component arcsine measure, using a zero-mass-energy lemma and
a circle-regularization argument, with an integrable factor retained
for the unbounded domain.

The comparison measure's potential inequality and energy computation
rest on elementary rational enclosures verified interval by interval.
The chapter's contract is the real bound: positivity of F_K(zeta(7))
with `log F_K(zeta(7)) <= U K^2 + O(K log K)` for `U = 44/25`, the
analytic half of the final exponent.

# Elementary enclosures

The rigorous Taylor enclosures for `log` and `arctan` with
explicit remainders below `2^{-144}`, with the argument
reductions for large and small inputs, the Machin-like formula
for `π`, and square-root enclosure by adjacent multiples of
`2^{-144}`.

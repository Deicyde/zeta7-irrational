# Andreief identity

Andreief's integration identity for moment determinants, in
the form that rewrites the determinant as a multiple integral
with a squared Vandermonde factor. No matching statement was
found in the pinned Mathlib checkout, so this is project work;
the paper notes it also follows by expanding both Vandermonde
determinants and integrating term by term.

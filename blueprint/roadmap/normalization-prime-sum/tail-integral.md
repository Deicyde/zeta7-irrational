# Tail integral bound

Write `R(x) = xF(x) + Q(x)` with the periodic coefficient `F`
and bounded remainder `Q`, bound `Q` between `-1/2` and `27/8`,
and prove the tail estimate
`∫_M^∞ R(x)/x^3 dx ≥ -λ/M + 11549/(720M^2) - 44/M^3`
by double integration by parts against the explicit periodic
primitives `P` and `C` with `|C| < 22`.

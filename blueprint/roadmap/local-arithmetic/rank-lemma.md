# Rank lemma

For matrices with `v_p(A_{ij}) ≥ w_i + w_j` for nonpositive
half-integer weights and an integral `L` of rank at most `r`
with exactly `z` zero weights,
`v_p det(A + p^{-1} L) ≥ 2∑ w_i - min(r,z)`.
Pure linear algebra over a valued field; no project definitions
needed for the statement.

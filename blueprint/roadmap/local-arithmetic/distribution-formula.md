# Distribution formula

For odd primes `p ≥ 11` with the parameter choice `Y = p^7 X + C_p`,
the distribution identity
`τ_X(g) = p^{-6} ∑_{a=0}^{p-1} τ^ext_Y(g(a+pz))` for rational
functions with polynomial part and simple integer poles, together
with the bound `C_p ∈ p^7 ℤ_p`.

The proof evaluates the analytic expansion on poles, kills the
`e = 0` term by the vanishing character sum and the `e ≥ 1` terms
by valuation, checks polynomials by Bernoulli multiplication and
`g = 1/x` directly, then extends by the shared reflection and
difference identities.

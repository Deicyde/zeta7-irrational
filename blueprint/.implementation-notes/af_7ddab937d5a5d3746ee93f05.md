## Evidence

For `p > K` every entry `Gentry n i j` already has `p`-integral
coefficients (checked `n = 1,2,3`, all primes `K < p < 6K`), but single
residues have `v_p = -1`: poles `a` and `p - a` (both in `(N, K]`) must
be paired. So the paper's sentence "the residues are `p`-integral" is
false as written, while the conclusion holds. For `p > K` no prime
above 37 divides any coefficient of the exact `Delta_K(X)` at `n = 1`
(checked to 400). The residue-level data for the pairing moved to the
paired-pole congruence note on 2026-10-18.

Proof route: the polynomial part has integer coefficients and degree
`<= 7N + h - 2 < 3p - 4`; for unpaired `a`, `Dtail'(-a^2)` is a
`p`-unit; each pair is the paired-pole congruence with `S = (N, K]`
and `Q = Rnum`.

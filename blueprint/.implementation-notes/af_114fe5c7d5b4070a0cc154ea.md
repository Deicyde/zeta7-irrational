## Evidence

Independent recheck (2026-10-18, exact integer arithmetic over class
counts, doubled weights) for every `n <= 60` and every prime satisfying
(52), 7040 cases: the zero count `z` equals `p - 1 - N + u` for
`K < 2p` and `p + u` for `K >= 2p`; `sum w - min(r_p, z)` equals
`gamma^out_p` in all 5171 cases with `K < 2p` and exceeds it by exactly
3 in all 1869 cases with `K >= 2p`. The earlier outer-estimate note
reported an excess of 4 at `n = 1`, `p = 17`; with `r_p = 11` the
excess there is 3 (an excess of 4 is what the actual rank 10 of `L`
gives in place of `r_p`).

The per-class contributions in the article (doubled sum `-9(l - 2)`
and two zeros for `delta = 0`; `0, -1, -2, -4, -6` and `1, 1, 2, 2, 3`
zeros for `delta = 1`, `l = 2..6`) give the closed forms directly; the
proof is a finite case analysis on `l_K(a)` plus the two class-count
sums.

check): the polynomial moments below `3p - 4` are `p`-integral at every

## Reuse path on main

For `v_p(mu(t^e)) >= -1`, reuse Lemma 3.4 from
`ZetaSeven/Completion.lean` on main: `ZetaSeven.kappa_eq`
(`kappa d = d.descFactorial 5 * bernoulli (d - 5) / 720`) and
`ZetaSeven.kappa_padicValRat_ge` (`-1 <= padicValRat p (kappa d)` for
`p >= 7`). The bridge `muMonomial e = (-1)^e * kappa (2e + 7)` follows
from `kappa_eq`, or from `ZetaSeven.tauPoly_monomial_pullback` with
`b = 1` (`ZetaSeven/TauPullback.lean`), since `kappa d` is
`tauPoly (X ^ d)`.

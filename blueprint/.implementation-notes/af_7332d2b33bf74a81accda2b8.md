## Suggested Lean definition

Define `g` by the explicit formula of the article, not as a limit, so
the definition needs no existence proof:

```
alpha = 3/40, lambda = 37/40; for 1/3 < y <= 1:
  k = floor(1/y), v = 1 - k*y, u = max(0, alpha + v - y),
  t = min(alpha, v) + u, r = min(lambda, max(0, 1 + 6*alpha - 3*y))
  g y = 9*(1 - y) - 8*t + min(r, y - alpha + u)            if y > 1/2
  g y = 9*(1 - y) - 16*alpha - 7*t + min(r, y + u)         if y <= 1/2
g y = 0 for 1 < y < 2*lambda
```

Then `R0 y = g y + d y` with `d` from (72) (the outer positive part
times the indicator of `(1/3, 1/2)`), and `I_out` the integral (73)
over `[1/3, 37/20]`; its integrand reduces to `g y + scalar y`, with
`scalar y = -2*lambda*floor(1/y) + sum_{j=1}^{7} max(0, 2*lambda - j*y)`.
Over `ℝ`, or over `ℚ` with a separate real-valued integral.

The limit property, `-gammaOut p = K * g (p/K) + O(1)` uniformly for
`K/3 < p <= 2h` (with `gammaOut = 0` for `p > K`), belongs to the proof
of the prime-sum bound, where Proposition 5.4 uses it; this node does
not prove it. It is bounded arithmetic on `floor(K/p)`, `K mod p` and
`min`/`max` of linear expressions, with `N = 3n`, `h = 37n`, `K = 40n`.

## Evidence

The formula for `g` was derived by scaling (54), (58), and (59) and
checked twice independently (2026-10-18, exact rationals): `g` is
continuous on `(1/3, 37/20)`, including at `1/2` and `1`, and the
integrand `g + scalar` is affine on each cell between
consecutive fractions `k/(40m)` with `m <= 12` (checked at four
interior points per cell). It has 11 affine pieces, listed in the
exact outer integral article, and integrates to
`I_out = 453803/288000`. Reading (71) literally (`R0 = g`, integrand
`g - d + scalar`) gives `445163/288000`, since the integral of `d`
is `3/100`; extending (59) to `p > K` instead of `gammaOut = 0` gives
`-635917/288000`. See source-map errata 8 and 9.

Evidence that does not depend on how `R0` and `d` are read: the direct
prime sum `-K^(-2) * sum_{K/3 < p <= 2h} L_p log p`, with
`L_p = v_p(S_K) + gammaOut p` from (59) and (62) and `gammaOut = 0` for
`p > K`, is `1.5717`, `1.5744`, `1.5751` at `K = 10^5, 10^6, 10^7`
(a reviewer's measurement, reproduced 2026-10-18 with a float sum over
a sieve). It approaches the adopted `I_out = 453803/288000 ≈ 1.5757`,
not (71)'s `445163/288000 ≈ 1.5457`.

Branch `formalize/outer-estimate` (`gammaOut`, commit `0f0a541`) has
no `p > K` case; see the outer range data note.

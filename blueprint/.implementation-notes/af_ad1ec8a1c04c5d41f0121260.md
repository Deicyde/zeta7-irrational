Gauss-valuation rank lemma: not formalized. Split out of the
outer-estimate node on 2026-10-18. The landed `ZetaSeven.rank_lemma`
covers only `Matrix (Fin h) (Fin h) ℚ` with doubled integer weights
(`w i + w j <= 2 * padicValRat p (A i j)`), so this is a genuine new
node, not a restatement.

## Route

From `ZetaSeven.rank_lemma` by expanding `det(A + p^{-1} L)` row-wise
(`Matrix.det_updateRow_add`, or `MultilinearMap.map_add_univ` on the
rows): each `X^k` coefficient is a sum of determinants of mixed
matrices (rows of the coefficient matrices of `A`) plus `p^{-1}` times
`L` with the rows that took an `X`-power zeroed; `Matrix.rank` of a
row-zeroed matrix is at most `Matrix.rank L`, and the rational rank lemma
applies to each. In the application `A` has degree at most one in `X`,
so a version for `A = alpha + X beta` would suffice, but the general
statement costs little more.

The paper's convention `v^G_p(0) = +inf` differs from the junk value
`0` used by `gaussVal` on branch `formalize/outer-estimate`; state the
conclusion coefficientwise (every coefficient has `padicValRat` at least
the bound, or is zero) to avoid the junk value.

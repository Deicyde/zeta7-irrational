## Finding: (53) is false as written, the estimate is not

The paper's (53) claims `A` is integral at every prime; it is not in
the monomial basis (details in the rank-decomposition note). The
proposition itself holds and is sharp. Exact `Delta_K(X)` at `n = 1`
(`K = 40`, `N = 3`, `h = 37`; degree 37, leading coefficient matches
(10)) has `v^G_p(Delta_K) = -138, -123, -130, -76, -58, -4` against
`gamma^out_p = -146, -129, -130, -76, -58, -4` for
`p = 17, 19, 23, 29, 31, 37`.
